One common quadratic.
For original fitting count c at the edited pixel and normalized edit u, the squared distance multiplied by 256 is 256u² - 32cu + b. The other observed coordinates determine the integer intercept b. The 256u² term is shared by every row and cancels when comparing them. Grouping the 17 possible source counts leaves at most 17 candidate slopes. Lower-intercept lines dominate equal slopes; coincident minima retain all tied indices.
A rational lower envelope gives the open intervals. A separate verifier recomputes every source coefficient, then checks every row against the chosen line at both interval endpoints. Affine differences that are nonnegative at both endpoints remain nonnegative inside. All-row endpoint evaluations recover complete ties, including lines pruned from the open-interval hull. A self-consistent witness is not proof that it used the intended source; this verifier also matches the supplied original rows and fixed query.
